# Planet albedo

Built-in imagegen; generated once, no variants or edits.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original high-resolution diffuse albedo texture for a physically based Three.js spherical destination planet, intended to visually match detailed Blender spacecraft and textured asteroids.
Primary request: A fictional realistic rocky ocean planet surface as a flat 2:1 equirectangular seamless planetary map, desired output 2048x1024 pixels. Full 360-degree longitude and 180-degree latitude coverage. This is the flat rectangular color texture for a sphere, not a picture of a sphere.
Composition: entire image filled by mapped planetary surface; horizontally seamless left and right borders; correctly stretched polar latitude texture along top and bottom; believable irregular archipelagos and geological continents with generous oceans.
Color and materials: slate blue oceans; muted teal and dark mineral continents; pale ice at polar latitudes and tan coasts; finely resolved natural geological terrain, erosion, coastlines and ocean depth variations; restrained natural colors.
Lighting: pure evenly illuminated diffuse albedo, with no directional lighting, no cast shadows, no specular highlights or baked global illumination.
Avoid: clouds, cloud shadows, atmosphere, glow, night side, terminator, spherical disc shape, stars, outer space, grids, borders, text, labels, watermarks, UI.
